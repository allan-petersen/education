The Terser notation 
STF-2(0)-1
STF-2(0)-2
STF-2(0)-3 
Refer to first, second and third components within repetition 0. 

STF-2(0)-1
STF-2(1)-1
STF-2(2)-1
Refer first component of repetition 1,2 and 3.

Repetiton seperator: ~ (single field with multiple values)
Component seperator: ^ (data field into several pieces)
